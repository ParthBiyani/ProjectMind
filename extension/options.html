ProjectMind capture

Pages you read are sent to the ProjectMind program on this computer, and nowhere else, except the sites on the never-capture list below. Pages on this computer or your local network, and pages that ask for a password or a card number, are never read.

Connection

Paste the result of projectmind daemon token. You only need to do this once; it is kept when the browser or the computer restarts.

Never capture

Nothing from these sites, or their subdomains, is ever read. Adding a site also forgets what was already read from it. The same list applies to browser history.
